# Component card: the coupon studio and the workspace | version 0.8 | updated 2026-11-08

## Purpose and intended use

The coupon FEA workspace at `/workspace/fea` (ADR-0062; `/chat`, `/chat/view`, `/studio` and `/studio/view` redirect there with their query), where an engineer states a coupon test, checks its setup, solves it and reads the result in a 3D view, without installing anything (ADR-0051). Three columns: one folding panel per step on the left, the stating steps numbered (1 Test and standard, 2 Project, 3 Geometry, 4 Layup and material, 5 Loads and supports, 6 Mesh; then Validate, Run, Outputs, and Advanced with every other row), under a line that says what is being set up (the test and the standard it follows, or none); the first step asks for the test and then for one of our standards that covers it, and the steps that depend on the test wait for it and show what the standard asks of their rows (ADR-0062 amendment 1), each editing named rows of the one values file; in the centre a sketch of the typed rows until the check, then the package's preview, then the fields after a run; on the right the coupon assistant, which offers one-click choices, reads a typed line into the rows, and names the panel that asks for the next thing. The front door `/workspace` lists the projects this browser keeps and starts a coupon or a laminate project; `/workspace/laminate` is the laminate workspace (ADR-0060; ADR-0062 part 5): Units, Material, Layup, Loads and Solve panels, the stack drawn ply by ply and the engine's answer in the centre, the laminate assistant beside them; `/workspace/cad` is a placeholder. It replaces the terminal for the four in-plane coupon tests (unnotched tension and compression, open-hole tension, in-plane shear), the two bending tests (three- and four-point bending, ADR-0053: the span, the overhang and the loading span as rows; the deflection along z, the rotations, the moments, the transverse shear forces and both faces of every ply in the viewer) and the `plate` test (ADR-0054: supports and loads over named regions, stated on the panel or by clicking an element of the model, pressures included). A coupon can be a plastic (ADR-0075): Material kind = isotropic on the Layup step gives its thickness, E and nu in place of the plies, and an unnotched tension coupon can be a dog-bone, its narrow section, fillets and Type IV's outer radius on the Geometry step, sketched as the engine meshes it and sized by ASTM D638-22's Types I to V. The viewer opens in a second window by the job's link, `/workspace/fea/view?job=<id>`.

## What it does and does not compute

Nothing. Every number the page shows is written by the fea package: the setup checklist and the preview (`POST /v1/coupon-jobs/check`), the fields with their display averages, the ranges of every field and the summary (`fields.json`, ADR-0063), and the template explanation (`explanation.json`), read as files of the job (ADR-0050 amendment 1); a laminate problem is solved by the engine through `POST /v1/laminates/solve` (ADR-0060). The page parses text into the values object, picks arrays, maps values to colours and draws (ADR-0001); the sketch draws typed rows only and prints no number it derives. The form's rows are the package's own definition, `lib/coupon-form-definition.json`, exported by `fea/tools/export_form_definition.py` and checked against the package by `fea/tests/test_studio_tool.py`. The explanation of a result may be rephrased by a language model behind a setting, every number checked against the engine's (ADR-0052); the assistants' readers are fixed patterns, not a model.

## Inputs / outputs

Input: a project (`lib/project-store.ts`), kept in this browser under `anisotropy.projects.v1` until the server store of ADR-0062 part 6 exists: for a coupon the rows of the coupon input form (ADR-0046) with the coupon's codes derived from the material, the test and the layup (ADR-0038 amendment 1), typed on the panels, picked from the library, loaded from the public example, read back from a coupon on the machine (`GET /v1/coupons/{name}/values`) or stated to the assistant; for a laminate the draft of the problem, each value with the unit it was typed in. The rows are stated in the unit system the engineer picks (mm-MPa, m-Pa, in-psi or in-ksi; ADR-0061): the labels, the chips, the tape estimate and the library figures the page shows in that system are written by the fea package into `lib/coupon-form-definition.json`, and the page converts nothing; the results, the checklist and the PDF come back in the same system, and the decks stay in mm-MPa (or m-Pa when stated so). Output: the checklist (thirteen items, each passed, warned, failed, missing or pending, with the rows it concerns and each panel's badge the worst of its items; the Test standard item lists each requirement of the standard picked with this coupon's value and what its clause asks, ADR-0072; the Test and standard panel's card shows the same before and after a check, and each later step lists the requirements that name its rows, and the Geometry panel offers the standard's sizes as buttons that copy the engine's figures into the rows), the preview of the model (the mesh with the held tab framed in violet and the pulled tab framed in amber with an arrow, lit for presentation), the job's stages, the fields (displacement, strains, section forces, every ply's stresses in material axes and its von Mises stress, the failure index and strength ratio by criterion; element values or display averages, deformed or not, coloured unlit on the graphics card with bands and an isoline at every legend tick, the legend's ends the package's), the summary (reaction, largest displacement, largest ply stresses, first-ply failure by criterion with the critical element framed in red, and for a coupon under a test standard the quantities the standard reduces a test to: area, stress, the extensometer's strain, the standard's modulus (D3039's chord, D638's modulus of elasticity over the narrow section) beside laminate theory and CalculiX, Poisson's ratio, the net section, the first-ply-failure stress; ADR-0072), the explanation, and links to every file of the job: the package zip, the PDF, the decks, the `.vtu`, the record, the JSON files. For a laminate: the A, B, D and Q matrices, the mid-plane strains, every ply's stresses, first-ply failure by each criterion and the explanation, in the units picked on the answer.

## Dependencies and connections

Next.js (static export), React Three Fiber and drei on three.js (MIT), loaded only in the browser (`next/dynamic` with `ssr: false` inside a client component, as the installed Next.js docs require). The API of ADR-0050 with amendment 1: the check, the job with its stage and files, the coupon listing; `POST /v1/laminates/solve` of ADR-0060. The actor is `studio`. The project and the run: `lib/project-store.ts`, `components/studio/project-context.tsx`, `components/studio/use-coupon-run.ts`, `components/studio/laminate-context.tsx`. The workspace: `lib/workspaces.ts`, `components/workspace/shell.tsx`, `chooser.tsx`, `fea-rail.tsx`, `laminate-rail.tsx`, `laminate-stage.tsx`, `panels/*`, `lib/workspace-panels.ts` (every row of the form in exactly one panel). Drawing code: `lib/studio-geometry.ts` (the plate with four vertices per element face, its deformed shape, the outline frames), `lib/studio-sketch.ts` (the typed rows as a sketch), `lib/studio-colors.ts` (one hue for a magnitude, two hues around a neutral grey for a signed field, fixed slots for ply angles; the scale's ends are the ranges the package writes, ADR-0063), `components/studio/field-material.ts` (the unlit shader that colours the results on the graphics card, with bands and isolines), `components/studio/scene-lighting.tsx` (the lit presentation mode of the model), `lib/studio-fields.ts` (which array a field shows), `lib/studio-form.ts`, `lib/studio-chat.ts`, `lib/laminate-chat.ts` and `lib/studio-api.ts`.

## Out of scope and limits

One job at a time (ADR-0050). Hosted, the site is behind Cloudflare Access on the invite list (ADR-0056); the projects are kept in the browser only, so clearing the site's data loses them and another machine does not see them, until the server store of ADR-0062 part 6. The 3D view needs WebGL 2; without it the page says so and shows the numbers, the checklist, the summary and the explanation. A job's status is lost when the API stops unless run records are on, where the jobs table keeps it (ADR-0056). The colours carry no number: the legend's ticks and the readout do, and the legend's ends are the package's (ADR-0063). The assistants read fixed phrases; a message that names no value is answered with the panel that asks for the next thing, not with a guess. CAD geometry is a placeholder tile.
