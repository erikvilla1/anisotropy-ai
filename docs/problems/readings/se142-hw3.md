# Reading: SE142/253A Homework 3, laminate stiffness and bending

The reading of every Homework 3 case (the note's file name is a prefix of theirs). The numbers quoted are the ones in the case files; the documents' tables are recomputed at every build.

## What the set exercised

- Problem 1, course reader 5.3: the moments that flatten a laminate, a prescribed-curvature problem with known forces (section 4 of the theory, the forces-and-curvature solver).
- Problem 2, course reader 5.6: the curvature kappa_x of two layups, [30/30/0]s and [30/-30/0]s, which differ only in the sign of the second ply and so in their bending-twisting coupling.
- Problem 3(a): A, D and the inverse of A for a symmetric [0_2/45/90/-45]s boron/epoxy laminate; 3(b) to (d): the unsymmetric version, where B is not zero and the full ABD inverse is needed. The 3(b) to (d) case carries 91 compared values, the largest in the set.
- Problem 4: the force Ny that flattens the boron laminate, again a mixed condition.

## What explains the differences

All compared values agree within the 0.2 percent tolerance. The course's Excel input files give the boron/epoxy shear modulus as 7,800,000 psi; the course property table says 0.78 Msi, and only 780,000 psi reproduces the key's A66, so the cases use 780,000 and the corrected Excel copies under `.local/homework/code/HW3/` do the same. The key prints the d matrix to four decimal places, about two significant figures, so d is not recorded.

## Verified, and not

- Verified by these cases: the A, B and D matrices with a nonzero B, the inverse blocks a, b and d, prescribed curvature with known forces, and the mixed solver with a held curvature.
- Not covered: nothing in this set is outside the engine; the key's two-figure d values are left out rather than compared loosely.
