# Reading: SE142/253A Homework 5, bending, cooling and first-ply failure

The reading of the Homework 5 case (the note's file name is a prefix of it). The numbers quoted are the ones in the case file; the document's tables are recomputed at every build.

## What the set exercised

- Problem 1: a [45/-45/90/0_2]s carbon/epoxy laminate under a biaxial load, a bending moment and cooling by 275 degrees F. The case checks the A and D matrices, the thermal resultants, the mid-plane strains and curvatures, the top-face stress of ply 9 in fiber axes, and part D: the value of Nx at which the first ply fails while Ny = Nx / 2 grows with it and the moment and the cooling stay fixed (ADR-0017, fixed and variable loading). The key gives 568.25 lb/in, ply 3, bottom face, transverse tension; the engine reproduces the load, the ply, the face position and the mode.

## What explains the differences

All 28 compared values agree within the 0.2 percent tolerance. The course file enters the compressive strengths as negative numbers; the engine takes strengths as positive magnitudes (the case says so).

## Verified, and not

- Verified by this case: the combination of mechanical loads, a moment and a temperature change; ply face stresses under bending; and first-ply failure by the maximum stress criterion with part of the loading held fixed, including which ply, which face and which mode governs.
- Not covered: Problem 2 is micromechanics (fiber and matrix properties to lamina properties), which the engine does not model and whose inputs are not in the provided files.
