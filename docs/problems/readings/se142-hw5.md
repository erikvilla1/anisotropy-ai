# Reading: SE142/253A Homework 5, bending, cooling, first-ply failure and micromechanics

The reading of the Homework 5 cases (the note's file name is a prefix of theirs). The numbers quoted are the ones in the case files; the documents' tables are recomputed at every build.

## What the set exercised

- Problem 1: a [45/-45/90/0_2]s carbon/epoxy laminate under a biaxial load, a bending moment and cooling by 275 degrees F. The case checks the A and D matrices, the thermal resultants, the mid-plane strains and curvatures, the top-face stress of ply 9 in fiber axes, and part D: the value of Nx at which the first ply fails while Ny = Nx / 2 grows with it and the moment and the cooling stay fixed (ADR-0017, fixed and variable loading). The key gives 568.25 lb/in, ply 3, bottom face, transverse tension; the engine reproduces the load, the ply, the face position and the mode.
- Problem 2 (two cases, parts a and c): the lamina constants of IM7/977-3 from the fiber and matrix constants at fiber volume ratios 0.65 and 0.58 (section 9 of the theory, ADR-0034). The key's E1 and nu12 are the rule of mixtures, its G12 the series model, and its E2 the series model with the constrained matrix modulus; all eight values agree within 1.5 percent, which is the key's two to three significant figures.

## What explains the differences

- Problem 1: all 28 compared values agree within the 0.2 percent tolerance. The course file enters the compressive strengths as negative numbers; the engine takes strengths as positive magnitudes (the case says so).
- Problem 2: the course's Lecture Pack 2, with its fiber and matrix tables and its "hybrid Voigt-Reuss" model, is not in the repository. The cases take the constituent constants from Daniel and Ishai Tables A.2 and A.3 (pp. 375-376) and name the models that reproduce the key. The remaining differences (E2 8.78 against 8.76, nu12 0.2525 against 0.25) are within the key's rounding; which model the course calls hybrid Voigt-Reuss is UNVERIFIED until the lecture notes are on hand.

## Verified, and not

- Verified by these cases: the combination of mechanical loads, a moment and a temperature change; ply face stresses under bending; first-ply failure by the maximum stress criterion with part of the loading held fixed, including which ply, which face and which mode governs; and the rule of mixtures, the series models and the constrained series model against the key's numbers.
- UNVERIFIED: the course's model definition and its constituent tables (Lecture Pack 2); the comparison of the predictions with the measured IM7/977-3 constants the key quotes in part b (190 GPa, 9.9 GPa, 0.35, 7.8 GPa) is a statement about the models, not about the engine, and is not a case.
