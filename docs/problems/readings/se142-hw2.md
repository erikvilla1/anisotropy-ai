# Reading: SE142/253A Homework 2, the lamina

The reading of every Homework 2 case (the note's file name is a prefix of theirs). The numbers quoted are the ones in the case files; the documents' tables are recomputed at every build.

## What the set exercised

- Problem 1: the compliance of a single lamina at 45 degrees, in fiber axes and laminate axes. The engine's rotation functions (section 2 of the theory) produce every entry the key states.
- Problem 2, course reader 3.8: a 30-degree lamina compressed by 1 percent between frictionless rigid walls, and the same without walls. These are mixed load and deformation conditions: a strain is imposed in one direction, the wall holds the strain in the other, and the shear stress is zero. The engine's mixed solver (section 4) returns the wall stress and the free deformations; the stress that compresses the lamina, 282 MPa in the key, is reproduced.
- Problem 3(b): a stress given in the lamina's fiber axes, rotated to the laminate axes.
- Problem 4(a): the transformed stiffness and compliance of a 20-degree lamina; 4(c): the fiber-direction strains at which the lamina fails, from the strengths and the maximum stress criterion.

## What explains the differences

None of the compared values lies outside the case tolerance of 0.2 percent, which is the rounding of the key's four significant figures. The course Excel files for Problem 4 carry swapped titles (the P4ab file holds the P4c inputs and the reverse); the cases take their numbers from the key, not from the titles.

## Verified, and not

- Verified by these cases: ply stiffness and compliance, rotation of stiffness, compliance, stress and strain, the mixed solver with an imposed strain and a held strain, and the maximum stress criterion on a single lamina.
- Not covered: Problem 3(a) and 4(b), which the key answers with derivations rather than numbers.
