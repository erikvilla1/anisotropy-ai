# Reading: SE142/253A Homework 4, temperature and hybrid laminates

The reading of every Homework 4 case (the note's file name is a prefix of theirs). The numbers quoted are the ones in the case files; the documents' tables are recomputed at every build.

## What the set exercised

- Problem 2, course reader 5.13: an unsymmetric laminate after cure, so the temperature change produces thermal moments and the laminate curves with no mechanical load (section 7 of the theory).
- Problems 3(a) and 3(b): the thermal expansion of IM7 laminates, that is the laminate's own expansion coefficients from the ply values.
- Problems 4(a) and (b): a hybrid laminate of IM7 tape and AGP370 woven fabric, two materials and two ply thicknesses, under an in-plane load and cooling; the key reports the fiber-axis strain and stress in seven of the eleven plies, all reproduced. Problem 4(c): the same laminate in bending.

## What explains the differences

All compared values agree within the 0.2 percent tolerance. The course's Excel input files for Problem 4 repeat ply number 9; the second of the two is ply 8, as the case states, and the corrected Excel copies under `.local/homework/HW4/ours/` renumber it. With a temperature change the ply stresses come from the mechanical strain, the total strain minus the free thermal strain of each ply; the key and the engine agree on that convention.

## Verified, and not

- Verified by these cases: thermal resultants and thermal moments, the thermomechanical response, ply stresses under a temperature change, and laminates with several materials and thicknesses (ADR-0015).
- Not covered: no part of this set is outside the engine.
