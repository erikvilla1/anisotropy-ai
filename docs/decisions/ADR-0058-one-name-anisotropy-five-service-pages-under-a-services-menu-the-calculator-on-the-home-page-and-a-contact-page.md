## Context

The founder audited the website on 2026-10-31 (layout and interfaces, not copy). The header then read About, Industries, Services, Materials library, Calculator, Studio and the brand "Anisotropy AI". The audit found Services, Calculator and Studio in conflict ("the menu is not intuitive"), the calculator "too simple" for a tab of its own, and the studio a finite element tool that belongs under Services. It asked for one page per service, each showing what the service does and a demonstration first, with access below, and for a Contact page. It also wants access to the "expensive" services granted on creating an account and limited to a number of uses, and asks for a meeting on that; the founder deferred that part on 2026-11-05.

Facts that bound the choice: `/studio`, `/studio/view` and `/chat` are the routes ADR-0051 and ADR-0055 decided, the mocked and live browser tests open them by path, and the hosted live test of ADR-0056 opens `/studio` on anisotropy.dev. The CI job `calculator-browser` waits for `http://localhost:3001/calculator` before it runs. The site is a static export served by a Worker that treats only `/api` specially, so a nested route exports as a page of its own. The whole hostname sits behind Cloudflare Access during the closed beta, so no page is public yet.

## Options considered

1. Keep "Anisotropy AI" and the flat six-link header; add the service pages beside the existing tabs. Rejected: the audit's first two items ask for the opposite, and three overlapping entry points (Services, Calculator, Studio) are what it calls unintuitive.
2. One long `/services` page with an anchor per service. Rejected: the audit asks for a page per service with its own description, demonstration and access section, and a single page cannot carry five demonstrations.
3. Five routes `/services/<slug>` under a disclosure in the header; `/calculator` and `/studio` kept at their routes but out of the header, the studio reached from the finite element page's access section and the calculator from the home page. Chosen.
4. Move `/studio` under `/services/finite-element-modeling`. Rejected for now: it breaks the routes of ADR-0051 and ADR-0055, every studio browser test and the hosted live test, for no gain the audit asks for; the finite element page links to the studio instead.
5. Build account sign-up and per-account use limits now. Rejected by the founder on 2026-11-05 until the team has met; the hosted tier's per-person job limits (ADR-0056, stage 5) already cover the API side.

## Decision

- The name is "Anisotropy" everywhere a visitor sees it: the wordmark, the page titles, the footer, the aria labels, the README and the package name. Decision records, skills, `docs/brain/` and the git history keep the wording they were written with.
- The header is About | Industries | Materials library | Services | Contact us. Services is a link to the landing page plus a disclosure button listing the five service pages: `/services/computer-aided-design`, `/services/finite-element-modeling`, `/services/test-plans`, `/services/documentation`, `/services/manufacturing-plans`, in the audit's order, from one data module `lib/services.ts`. On narrow screens the five appear inside the open mobile menu.
- Each service page has the same shape: the description, what exists today, a demonstration, what is planned (labelled PLANNED), then the access section. Nothing on a page claims more than the repository backs; the one number quoted is the IM7/8552 bending comparison already in the changelog (ADR-0053).
- The finite element page's access section links to `/studio` for the invited users of the closed beta; the documentation and design pages do the same; test plans send the visitor to `/chat`; manufacturing plans, which do not exist, send them to `/contact`.
- `/calculator` stays as a route out of the header; the home page carries the calculator (its own change). `/studio`, `/studio/view`, `/chat`, `/data` and `/sources` do not move.
- `/contact` shows the founder's address as a plain mailto link until an anisotropy.dev address exists. The site is static; there is no form.
- Pictures and clips are slots with a label (`components/media-slot.tsx`); the team supplies the media later. No file is fetched from outside the site.
- Accounts and per-account use limits are not built; they wait for the team meeting the audit asks for.

## As built (2026-11-05)

Four pull requests, in the order the plan set: the site map (the name, the header with the Services disclosure, `/services` and its five pages, `/contact`, the media slots); the engine and API description of a stack (ADR-0059); the About page with the workflow and the roadmap moved from the home page and the team at its end, plus the Industries media slots; and the home page with the laminate calculator up front (`components/home/`), the chat block under it, and five blurbs (mission, the five services, the evidence, the team, what is new). The home page draws the typed stack with react-three-fiber and asks the engine for every number per settled edit; "Want to check the math?" hands the inputs to `/calculator`, kept as the full solution. Differences from the plan: the Services item is a link plus a disclosure button rather than a menu role, so the existing navigation test keeps its "Services" link; the layup reader is the coupon chat's, so a layup may list as many plies as the coupon form has rows; the ABD grid shows A, B and D once each with a note that B appears twice in the ABD matrix.

## Why (evidence)

- The audit of 2026-10-31, revised 2026-11-01, is the founder's; its items 1, 2, 3, 13, 14 and 15 are quoted above. The founder's four answers of 2026-11-05 are recorded in the Decided by line.
- The routes that must not move: `tests/browser/studio.spec.ts` opens `/studio` and `/studio/view`; `tests/browser/studio-live.spec.ts` opens `/studio`; `playwright.studio.config.ts` and `playwright.studio-live.config.ts` wait for `/studio`; `playwright.calculator.config.ts` waits for `/calculator`.
- The Worker's only special path is `/api` (`worker/routing.ts`), so the nested service routes need no routing change; the static export writes `out/services/<slug>.html` (checked by `npm run build` in the evaluation note of this change).
- Test runs, in [the evaluation note](../evaluations/website/2026-11-05-site-map.md): `npm test` 114 passed; `npm run typecheck` and `npm run typecheck:worker` clean; the build exports 19 routes with the five service pages and `/contact`; the studio browser test reaches the studio through the finite element page (7 passed, 1 failure that pull request #115 fixes); the calculator browser test passes by route (7 passed); the local specs 12 passed, with one failure in the physics search count that predates the branch.

## Consequences

- Easier: one data module names the services, so the header, the landing page, the five pages and the home page agree; a planned service is visibly planned; the studio keeps its address and tests.
- Harder: two places now describe the finite element work (the service page and the studio itself), and the service page's facts must be kept true as the studio changes; the Access policy still hides every page, so the public cannot see the new map until the policy is scoped by path (a later ADR-0056 amendment).
- We depend on: the audit's structure holding until the team meeting; each team member's consent before a name appears on the site (ADR-0057 amendment 1).

## How we will know it was wrong

- The team asks for Calculator or Studio back in the header, or visitors cannot find the studio from the services pages (a support question or a beta tester's report says so).
- A service page stays "planned" for a quarter with no change behind it, which means the page promises more than the roadmap holds.
- The team meeting decides on accounts and limits in a shape the access sections cannot express, which means the pages need a new section rather than a link.

## Amendment 1, 2026-11-06 (the chat and the studio move to `/workspace`; the old addresses redirect)

**Context.** This record froze `/chat`, `/chat/view`, `/studio` and `/studio/view` and pointed every access section at `/chat`. [ADR-0062](ADR-0062-the-chat-becomes-a-workspace-product-a-solver-independent-project-three-columns-one-panel-per-step-the-assistant-beside-it.md) makes the product a workspace: a chooser at `/workspace`, the coupon FEA workspace at `/workspace/fea` with its viewer at `/workspace/fea/view`, the laminate response at `/workspace/laminate`, and a CAD placeholder at `/workspace/cad`.

**Options.** (1) Keep `/chat` as the workspace's address: rejected, the word no longer says what the page is, and the chooser is not a chat. (2) Move to `/workspace` and let the old addresses break: rejected, saved links, the service pages and the viewer links of running jobs carry them. (3) Move, and keep the four old addresses as redirects that carry their query, as amendment 3 of ADR-0055 did for `/studio`: chosen.

**Decision.** Option 3. `app/chat/page.tsx`, `app/chat/view/page.tsx`, `app/studio/page.tsx` and `app/studio/view/page.tsx` render `RedirectTo` (`components/redirect-to.tsx`) towards `/workspace/fea` and `/workspace/fea/view`, so `?material=`, `?prompt=` and `?job=` arrive where they are read. The header's call to action reads "Open the workspace" and goes to `/workspace`; the service pages' access sections go to `/workspace/fea` ("Open the workspace", "Describe a coupon"); the materials page's "Discuss this material", the home composer, the data page and the industries page follow. The header and the footer treat `/workspace/*` as `/chat` was treated: the short header, no footer. `tests/site-map.test.ts` admits `/workspace` and its pages as access targets.

**Evidence.** `npm run typecheck`: clean; `npm test`: 142 passed; `npx playwright test --config playwright.studio.config.ts`: 13 passed (the eleven studio scenarios on the new routes, the two workspace scenarios); the site-wide scenarios `expansion`, `portfolio` and `home` against `serve out`: 15 passed; `STUDIO_LIVE=1 npx playwright test --config playwright.studio-live.config.ts`: 1 passed (1.1 min), the example coupon checked and solved from the left column of `/workspace/fea`.

## Amendment 2, 2026-11-12 (one title template, plain descriptions, a 404 page, a shared image, and the old addresses answered by the Worker)

**Context.** The site audit of 2026-11-12 (findings shell-1 to shell-5, shell-10, shell-13, shell-14 and shell-20) found: titles with two separators ("About — Anisotropy", "Workspace | Anisotropy"), and `/library`, `/data` and the 404 under the home's title; a root description, "An engineering intelligence platform … Explore the first chapter.", inherited by every page without its own; descriptions that read as change notes on the old addresses or named a solver on `/workspace/fea`; the framework's white 404 page without `#main`; the four old addresses redirecting in the browser after a flash of the marketing header, `/studio` titled "Chat"; no shared image, favicon or theme colour; and KaTeX's stylesheet on every page.

**Options.** Titles: (1) a full title on each page, as before: rejected, the two separators show they drift; (2) one template in the root layout and the bare name on each page: chosen. The old addresses: (1) the redirect in the browser only, as amendment 1 built it: rejected, it needs a script and shows the wrong header first; (2) the hosted Worker answers them with a 301 that keeps the query, the client pages staying as the fallback where no Worker runs: the audit's fix, chosen. The shared image: (1) a drawn image with the wordmark and "Composites. Calculated." on #0d0d0d, as the audit proposed: rejected, it would add to the lead's approved artwork (ADR-0080) without the lead; (2) the approved logo cut to 1200 × 630: chosen.

**Decision.**
- **Titles:** `app/layout.tsx` sets the default "Anisotropy | Composites. Calculated." and the template "%s | Anisotropy"; each page's title is its bare name, the 404's "Page not found", and the old addresses carry their new page's title and description (`/studio` is "Coupon FEA").
- **Descriptions:** the root's is "Composite laminates and coupons, calculated: a cited materials library, a laminate calculator and coupon finite element analysis, with every number traced to its source."; `/industries`, `/workspace`, `/workspace/fea`, `/workspace/cad` and `/workspace/fea/view` get their own, in the copy style guide's words.
- **The 404 page,** `app/not-found.tsx`: the site's header, an opening on the page ground with `main#main`, "Page not found", one sentence, Home and Workspace. The Worker already serves it with status 404 (`not_found_handling`).
- **Shared image and icons:** `metadataBase` is `https://anisotropy.dev`; Open Graph type and site name, and a large Twitter card. `app/opengraph-image.jpg` is the approved logo on black (`public/assets/brand/Logos/Anisotropy Logo approved-04.jpg`, 8000 × 4500) cut 4280 × 2247 around the logo's centre and scaled to 1200 × 630, so the logo spans about 55 % of the width, nothing added; `app/favicon.ico` is `anisotropy-icon.svg` at 16, 32 and 48 px; the theme colour is #0d0d0d.
- **The old addresses:** `wrangler.jsonc` runs the Worker first for `/chat`, `/chat/*`, `/studio` and `/studio/*`; `movedLocation` in `worker/routing.ts` sends the four pages to `/workspace/fea` and `/workspace/fea/view` with their query, and `worker/index.ts` answers with a 301. Everything else under those paths goes on to the static files, so an unknown address there gets the 404 page with the site's header (`isAppPath` in `lib/site-nav.ts` names the same four, `MOVED_ADDRESSES`). The fallback pages say "Opening Coupon FEA…", get the workspace's header, and move the focus to the new page's content once it is there, with no focus ring round the whole page.
- **KaTeX's stylesheet** loads with `components/math-formula.tsx`, the one component that typesets, not from the root layout.
- **Not done here:** a noindex on the old addresses, since the founder deferred the indexing policy (decision D12, "later"); a title and description for `/library` and `/data`, whose client pages need a server layout in the library's own folder, left to the library's fix group.

## Amendment 4, 2026-11-13 (the Services drop-down is hidden for now; Services is a plain link)

**Context.** The lead, looking at the deployed header: "lets get rid of the dropdown for services. just hide the feature for now." A first build took the whole item out of the header; the lead corrected it the same day: "where did services go? just get rid of the + drop down is what I meant". The drop-down was the founder's request in the audit this record began with ("leave the tab called Services and make it a drop-down menu"), so this reverses it for the time being and is the founder's to accept with the pull request.

**Options considered.** (1) Keep "Services" in the header as a plain link to `/services`, without the plus and the panel, behind one switch: chosen, as the lead's correction says. (2) Take the item out of the header: built first on a misreading, and withdrawn. (3) Delete the panel's code: rejected, the lead said "for now".

**Decision.** `SERVICES_PANEL_IN_HEADER` in `lib/site-nav.ts` is `false`: the header lists About, Industries, Resources, Services and Contact us as five plain links, at every width, and the folded menu no longer lists the five services under Services. The link is marked as the current section on a service's own page, as the panel's link was. The five service pages, the footer's Services column (it reads `siteNav`, the whole list) and the home page's Platform cards stay. Setting the switch to `true` brings the plus and the panel back. The three browser cases that test the panel in the header are skipped while the switch is off, and one new case checks the plain link, wide and folded, and that the footer still lists the five pages.

**Evidence.** On 2026-11-14 against the dev server: `shell.spec.ts`, `expansion.spec.ts`, `home.spec.ts` and `intro.spec.ts`: 22 passed, 3 skipped (the three cases above), 0 failed. `npm test`: 299 tests, 298 passed, 1 skipped. `npx tsc --noEmit` clean. Screenshots at 1440 px and of the folded menu at 390 px were looked at: five links in the middle of the page, no plus; one Services row in the folded menu. UNVERIFIED: whether the founder agrees.
