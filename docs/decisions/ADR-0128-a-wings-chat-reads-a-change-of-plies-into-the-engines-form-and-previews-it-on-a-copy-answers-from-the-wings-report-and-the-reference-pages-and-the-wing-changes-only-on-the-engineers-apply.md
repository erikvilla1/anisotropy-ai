## Context

- The founder's ask names two things the wing page could not do: a change in words ("Add 2 0 plies to the spar caps from the root to 40% span") and questions about the wing being analysed. The wing now holds what the ask points at: spar caps and zones along the span (ADR-0125), the design rules at each layup and ply drop (ADR-0123), the strengths with their basis and page (ADR-0126) and three reference pages (ADR-0127).
- Every number of the product comes from the engine (ADR-0001). The coupon chat lets a model propose rows only through a guard that keeps what the engineer's words hold, and the engineer ticks a card before anything changes (ADR-0088).
- A wing file's laminates are symmetric and written by their outer half (ADR-0125). A ply added to one half is a pair in the laminate.
- The wing's versions live in the browser, each saying who made it and why (ADR-0122); going back is itself a version.
- The MCP server is read and preview only (ADR-0107); the client's own model writes the words.

## Options considered

1. **A fixed form for a change (`PlyChange`: add or take plies of one angle, one part, a stretch of the half span, in pairs, beside the plies of that angle or at the middle) that the engine makes on a copy of the wing file and works before and after; the page reads the form from the words by its own patterns, every number from the message; questions answered from the report the page holds and the reference pages; Apply makes a new version; the MCP server gets the same reads and the preview, never an apply** (chosen).
2. A model on the hosted site reads every wing message now, with a source guard as for the coupon. Not chosen for this step: the founder's asks are read by patterns exactly and free, the model's reader for the wing is a later step once the banks show the asks patterns miss, and a model is never needed to compute or apply anything.
3. A change written as a whole new half stack or zone table by the page or a model. Rejected: the engineer would have to read a table to see what changed; the engine's own sentence ("Two 0° plies added to the spar caps from the root to 40 % of the half span, beside the 0° plies: [0/0/0/45/-45/0]s there") is what is ticked, and the zones are split and joined by one function the tests pin.
4. A tool that applies a change from the MCP server or the chat. Rejected: the founder's plan has none; a saved wing changes only on the engineer's tap.
5. A ply count of one half. Rejected: engineers count the plies of the laminate ("add 2 plies"); an odd count cannot stay symmetric and is refused with the reason.

## Decision

- **The engine** (`wing/changes.py`): `PlyChange` (action, part of skin, spar or spar_caps, angle from −90° to 180°, an even count of 2 to 40, start before end as fractions of the half span, placement beside or middle). `change_wing` writes the wing file the change gives on a copy: the part's half stack when the change covers the whole span, else zones split at the change's edges and joined where neighbours hold the same stack, no zone repeating the part's own. Plies are added after the first run of plies of their angle (at the middle when there is none, or when asked) and taken from those nearest the middle, so the outside plies run on over the drop. `change_words` says the change in one sentence with the stacks it gives; `preview_change` works the wing as it is and as changed by hand and returns both summaries and the design rules of the part changed.
- **The API**: `POST /v1/wings/changes` (the wing as JSON or as its file's text, and the change) returns the preview; nothing is kept; a change the wing cannot take is refused in the engine's words; the record holds the change, its sentence and both summaries. `POST /v1/workspace/theory-sections` takes `about: "wing"` to search the wing's theory and the reference pages.
- **The page** (`lib/wing-chat.ts`, `wing-chat.tsx`, under the wing assistant's answer): a message is a change, a question of the results, of a design rule (met, or the wing's limit), of the strengths, of a layup, of why, how or which test, a command, or not read. A change is read into `PlyChange` with every number from the words; a stretch not named is the whole span and the card says so; a count, an angle, a part or a place it cannot read is asked once with an example; a rib or two parts at once are declined with where they are set. The preview card shows the engine's sentence, before and after (the lowest margin, each check's lowest margin, mass, the largest tip deflection, the first bending frequency) and the rules the change leaves unmet; Apply makes a new version "by the assistant" with the message, Dismiss leaves the wing as it is, and a preview of a wing changed since is not applied. Answers to questions are the report's numbers in the page's print; a question of why, how or which test shows the first section of our pages the search finds, with the wing's own facts on what it names; a section that names a tool of ours is not shown (ADR-0091).
- **The MCP server**: `read_wing`, `wing_results`, `wing_rules`, `wing_strengths`, `search_wing_references` and `preview_wing_change`, each reading a wing file on the engineer's machine by its path, or `example`; the instructions say where each number comes from and that nothing is applied.
- **The bank**: `tests/assistant-questions/wing.json` is live: 22 questions on the pages "wing" and "wing-caps", ten of them for an MCP client.

## Consequences

- The founder's ask works end to end on the page and from an MCP client, with no model on the page: every number is the engine's, every change the engineer's tap.
- A message the patterns cannot read gets examples, not an answer. Which asks they miss is what the bank and the live chat will show; that is when a model reader for the wing is built, held by the same kind of guard as the coupon's.
- A change is one part, one angle, one stretch at a time; a ±45° pair is two messages, each previewed.

## How we will know it was wrong

- Engineers' changes are refused or asked back more often than previewed on the live site: the patterns are too narrow and the model reader is due.
- A preview's sentence and the wing applied differ, or Apply overwrites a change the engineer made after the preview.
- An MCP client applies a change in the file without the engineer, or states a number no tool returned.
