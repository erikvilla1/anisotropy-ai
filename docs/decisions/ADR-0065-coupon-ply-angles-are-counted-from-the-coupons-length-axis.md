# ADR-0065: Coupon ply angles are counted from the coupon's length axis

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-08
- Decided by: Founder, 2026-11-08 ("0° along the load") | Drafted by: Claude Code
- Related: [ADR-0004](ADR-0004-sign-and-stacking-conventions.md) (unchanged: the engine's angle stays counterclockwise from the laminate x axis), [ADR-0003](ADR-0003-reject-invalid-input.md), [ADR-0030](ADR-0030-models-from-a-spec-calculix-as-reference-solver.md), [ADR-0035](ADR-0035-team-models-matched-node-for-node-and-a-short-comparison-with-a-share-folder.md), [ADR-0046](ADR-0046-coupon-intake-by-an-excel-input-form.md) (form version 0.7), [ADR-0055](ADR-0055-the-studio-opens-on-a-guided-chat-that-fills-the-same-form-rows-and-the-full-form-stays-one-click-away.md), [ADR-0056](ADR-0056-the-hosted-tier-runs-on-cloudflare-with-one-solver-image-r2-access-and-neon-postgres-invite-only.md); `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/spec.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/model.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/abaqus_reader.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/mesh.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/recovery.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/write_abaqus.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/write_calculix.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/convert_deck.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/equivalence.py`, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/intake.py`, `fea/tools/migrate_angles.py`, `fea/docs/theory.md`, `fea/tests/test_angles_from_length.py`, `fea/tests/test_migrate_angles.py`
- Area: fea

## Context

A coupon lies with its length along global y, the axis it is pulled or pushed along, its span in bending, its length as a plate; its width lies along x. The engine counts a ply's angle counterclockwise from the laminate x axis to the fibre (ADR-0004), and until this record the coupon flow passed the stated angles to the engine unchanged, so a coupon's 0° ply had its fibres across the width and its 90° ply along the pull: the form's hint read "0 = fibres across the coupon, 90 = fibres along the pull" (`intake.py`), the chat said "90° runs along the load", the deck writer wrote the global `*Orientation` with the stated angles, and `fea/docs/theory.md` stated the rule. The public record `fea/checks/unnotched-im7-8552/records/unnotched-im7-8552-nodal-calculix.toml:39-47` shows the consequence on the quasi-isotropic [45/0/-45/90]s coupon: ply 4, stated 90°, carries sigma_1 = 1180.67 MPa, the fibre stress of the ply along the pull. ASTM D3039 and the usual laminate notation count a coupon's 0° ply along the load; an engineer reading "[45/0/-45/90]s" expects the 0° plies to carry the pull. The founder decided on 2026-11-08 that a stated 0° ply runs along the load.

The deck reader accepted one orientation only, the global axes with zero rotation (`abaqus_reader.py`), so a team deck whose layup CSYS was turned was refused.

## Options considered

1. Keep counting from the width (x). Rejected by the founder: the stated layup reads the opposite of what the test standard and every engineer mean.
2. Rotate the mesh so the load runs along x. Rejected: the node sets, the probes, the tab lines, the team's decks and every record are laid out along y; every comparison with the team would turn with it.
3. Subtract 90° at spec load and keep writing global decks with the fibre angles on the layer lines. Rejected: an Abaqus user opening our deck would read 90, -45, 45, 0 on the layer lines of a coupon stated as [45/0/-45/90]s, and would have to add 90 in their head to see their own layup.
4. A per-spec switch, `angles_from = "load" | "width"`. Rejected: two code paths through every writer, reader and label, and "load" is the wrong word for bending (the span) and for a plate (its length), which share the axis but not the load.
5. Count from the length axis, state it in the spec (`angles_from = "length"`, required, no default, ADR-0003), and carry it in the deck as an `*Orientation` whose local direction 1 is global +y, with the stated angles on the layer lines. The reader derives each section's rotation from its orientation, and one function on each side (`spec.fibre_angles`, `model.fibre_angles`) adds the rotation for the engine. Chosen.

## Decision

Option 5. A coupon's ply angles are counted from its length axis, global y: the load axis in tension and compression, the span in bending, the length of a plate. The engine's convention (ADR-0004) does not change; a stated angle theta has its fibre at theta + 90° from global x, brought into (-90, 90] because a fibre has no arrow.

- `spec.Layup.angles_from: Literal["length"]` is required. A spec without it is refused with a message that names `fea/tools/migrate_angles.py`; it is never reinterpreted. `LENGTH_AXIS_ROTATION_DEG = 90.0`, `fibre_angle`, `fibre_angles(spec)` and `normalised_angle` live in `spec.py`; `spec_plies` builds the engine plies from the fibre angles; `full_angles` stays the stated angles, what every label prints.
- `write_abaqus` writes `*Orientation, name=Ori-1` / `0., 1., 0., -1., 0., 0.` / `3, 0.` (a = +y, b = z cross a = -x, a right-handed frame with axis 3 along +z) and the stated angles on the layer lines. `write_calculix` names each `*ORIENTATION` by the stated angle (`Ori-45` stays readable) and writes its axes from the fibre angle, with one comment line saying so; `convert_deck` does the same through the section's rotation. `femodel.PlyDefinition` carries `angle_deg` (stated) and `fibre_deg`; the deck heading reads "layup bottom-first [...] from the length axis (fibres from x: [...])".
- The reader accepts any right-handed frame in the plate's plane: a unit `a` with `a_z = 0`, `b = z cross a`, a rotation about axis 3 only; an out-of-plane `a`, a `b` that is not `z cross a`, an `a` that is not a unit vector and a rotation about axis 1 or 2 are refused by name. `ShellSection.rotation_deg` is the angle from global x to the orientation's `a` plus its rotation, 0 without an orientation. `model.fibre_angles(section)` is each expanded layer's angle plus that rotation, normalised; `section_plies`, both stiffness builders and `transverse_shear_stiffness` use it. The hand-made check decks keep the global `Ori-1`, so the closed-form cases do not change.
- Labels never read an engine ply angle: the kernel `Section.angles_deg` holds the stated angles, `recovery.PlyField.angles_deg` and `report.LaminateCheck.angles_deg` take them from there, and the fields, the preview, the checklist, the audit, the rebuild guide and the PDF print them. `compare.reference_quantities` and the CalculiX test rotate global stresses to the ply axes by the fibre angle.
- `equivalence` judges the layup by the fibre direction of each ply (stated angle plus rotation) and prints each deck's stated layup with its axis ("from the length axis (Ori-1, local 1 along y)", "from x (global axes)"); the "Shell orientation" row is listed, not judged.
- The coupon input form is version 0.7: the ply rows count from the length axis, the hints and the Definitions sheet say so, and a 0.6 values file is refused. `intake` writes `angles_from = "length"` into every spec it writes. Both chats count from the main axis and say so; `lib/coupon-form-definition.json` is refreshed from the package.
- The public check specs state `angles_from = "length"` and keep their layups as written ("[45/0/-45/90]s" with 0° along the pull), so their physical laminates turn by 90°; their decks, records, reading notes and browser fixtures are regenerated after this change. `fea/tools/migrate_angles.py` restates an older spec's angles by minus 90° into (-90, 90] and adds the key: the same physical laminate, so an old record still describes it.

## Why (evidence)

- The public record `fea/checks/unnotched-im7-8552/records/unnotched-im7-8552-nodal-calculix.toml:39-47` (written before this record): ply 4, stated 90°, carries sigma_1 = 1180.6732500000003 MPa, so under the old count the stated 90° plies were the ones along the pull.
- The founder's session of 2026-11-08: "0° along the load".
- `cd fea && uv run pytest -q tests/test_angles_from_length.py tests/test_spec.py tests/test_reader_rejections.py tests/test_recovery.py tests/test_write_abaqus.py tests/test_write_calculix.py tests/test_equivalence.py tests/test_femodel.py tests/test_migrate_angles.py`: a deck written from the public spec reads back to the spec's own engine plies; on the public QI coupon the stated 0° ply carries the largest fibre stress (above 1100 MPa at the centre element) and the stated 90° plies are compressed along their fibres; a stated [0]8 coupon under a pull with free edges carries sigma_1 = E1 eps_y and sigma_2 = tau_12 = 0 at the centre; a stated [90]8 coupon's reaction is E2/E1 of the [0]8 coupon's; the grips fixture counted from x equals the spec [90/-60/60/-60/90]s counted from the length axis, item by item; the reader accepts the y-aligned frame with rotation 90 and refuses the out-of-plane, non-unit, left-handed and axis-1 cases by name. The counts and the full output are in the pull request of this change.
- The sign of a layer angle under a rotated `*Orientation` in Abaqus itself (that a layer angle is counted counterclockwise about axis 3 from local direction 1, the frame our reader assumes) is UNVERIFIED in this repository until the team runs one regenerated deck. Our kernel and CalculiX are self-consistent through our own reader and writer regardless.

## Consequences

- An engineer states a coupon's layup as the test standard reads it, and our Abaqus deck shows the same numbers on its layer lines; the +90° lives in two functions and nowhere else.
- The reader now accepts a team deck whose layup CSYS is turned in the plane, which it refused before.
- The public coupons' physical laminates change: the QI coupon's reaction, displacements and centre strain stay (its A is isotropic), its per-ply rows permute 1 with 3 and 2 with 4, and the bending coupon's D changes materially. Every public deck, CalculiX record, reading note, case page, browser fixture and PDF is regenerated after this change; until then the deck-equality and record tests fail by design.
- The migration must precede the hosted deployment: `intake.existing_coupons` (`intake.py`) loads every spec under `.local/fea` to name a new coupon, and the hosted tier restores its coupons the same way, so one spec without `angles_from` blocks every job. `tools/migrate_angles.py` is run once on each older spec (the founder's `.local/fea/*/ours/*.toml`; the hosted coupons restored, migrated and synced back) before the new image is deployed; whether the hosted five are migrated or re-entered by their owners is the founder's call.
- The CalculiX orientation names no longer equal the axes they carry (`Ori-45` holds the fibres at -45° from x, the same line as 135°); the comment line in the deck says so. A converted deck whose sections state one layer angle under two rotations is refused.
- Notes under `docs/evaluations/` written before this record keep their dates and their words.

## How we will know it was wrong

- The team runs one regenerated public deck in Abaqus: S11 at the centre of the stated 0° ply of the QI coupon (`unnotched-im7-8552-nodal`) is about 1181 MPa and the stated 90° plies carry the small negative sigma_1. A result of 1181 MPa in the stated 90° ply instead would mean Abaqus counts a layer angle under a rotated `*Orientation` the other way, and the writer's frame or its layer-line sign must change.
- A user of the studio or the form reads a layup the other way round (asks why the 0° ply carries the load, or enters a coupon with 90 to mean along the pull): the hints have not reached them, and the words move.
