## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- The coupon input form explains itself (ADR-0046 amendment 1, form 0.2): every row says what it means, with an example, and a new sheet Definitions answers the first questions an engineer asks: the tests, which way 0 degrees points (across the coupon; 90 is along the pull), what an element size and a finer mesh do, library or own material, what G13 and G23 are and the usual estimate when none is measured, the grips, and where results are read.
- Seven decision records on the finite element method are Accepted by the founder (2026-10-26): ADR-0029 (our membrane solver, Gmsh, the Abaqus writer), ADR-0030 (models from a spec, CalculiX as the reference), ADR-0031 (a document per model comparison), ADR-0034 (micromechanics), ADR-0035 (our models match the team's node for node), ADR-0038 (coupon names) and ADR-0045 (Abaqus without the GUI). Their texts and amendments are unchanged.
- The document reviewer reads a package's whole PDF in one call; a page range needs pdftoppm, which a machine may not have (ADR-0047 amendment 1, from its first trial on the three X840 packages).
- The generated Abaqus decks run in Abaqus/Standard as written (ADR-0045 amendment 2): one variable per `*El Print` table, heading comments wrapped to Abaqus's 256 bytes, the `split_print_tables` option removed; reactions on nodal grips are now recorded (the ODB capitalises instance names). The committed decks and two record digests are regenerated.
- The rule for S12, accepted by the founder (ADR-0045 amendment 1): S12 is judged in the plies where it carries load once every section point is exported; until then the 0 degree faces take a floor of 4 % of the group scale, so differences below 0.04 % of the axial stress (the S4R against bilinear-membrane noise) do not count. The attempt-15 record of X840PW-UNT-L1 re-read with it passes 2404 of 2404. The rubric row keeps its text until its next version.
- Rubric 0.3, confirmed by the founder (ADR-0043 amendment 4): the row naming the files by digest and commit is gone (the form's Record sheet carries them); the evidence is the claim the team verifies, one value where the decks agree and no node offsets; where the team's export lacks a value (reaction, displacement and strain, first-ply failure, or S12 in the ply-stress row) the row states ours and the auditor judges it, the team's side left blank. Row B8 lists the team's boundary conditions and loads and ours in full, by reference point and set name. Section E (documentation) is off the rubric for now. The form says "Our engine", never "kernel", and names no decision record.
- Audit form v0.3 (ADR-0043 amendment 3, ADR-0044 amendment 1): the cover says the coupon, what is compared (in values, with the files each side comes from), the attempt, the sections with their meaning and how to fill it in; the Verdict fills itself from the Results; every table of the Results sheet says how to check its numbers from the package's files or in Abaqus; how the package was built (the team deck's digest, the engine's commit, who built it) moves to a hidden Record sheet and the headline numbers to the Results sheet. There is no Findings sheet: a failed row names its Cause, an Observation column takes the rest, and the verdict rule reads the rows alone. The Rubric sheet groups the rows under a banner per section and writes what our tools found in three columns, Team, Ours and Difference, one fact per line; the number sheets have number formats. The case page's audit block lists failed rows and observations.
- Rubric 0.2, confirmed by the founder (ADR-0043 amendment 1): the laminate-theory hand check D1 is held to the solver comparison's 1 % and applies only where the free length between the grips is at least three widths; a shorter coupon gets N/A with its distance from theory, the free length in widths and N_x/N_y at the centre stated. An item may carry `condition = { min_free_length_widths = n }`.
- Every coupon package uses the numbered folders `1-team-model/` (absent without a team model), `2-our-model/`, `3-material/` and `4-results/`, which now holds `ours-all-plies.csv` for every coupon (ADR-0043 amendment 2).
- The documents no longer quote the team's coupons or the course's problems (ADR-0042, C4): 18 evaluation notes move to `.local/notes/evaluations/`; the other notes, the ADRs, this file and the READMEs keep their text without those numbers and names. The tiny IM7/8552 check decks and the test deck helper become generic (25 by 100 mm, [0/30/-30/30/0]s, names of our own) and the three patch cases are recomputed with the engine. `paths.team_deck` finds a coupon's team deck by its place, so the local tests name no team file and read their expected counts from our model.

- The 23 course cases leave `engine/validation/cases/` for `.local/homework/<set>/ours/`, where `homework.py` now finds a set's problems (ADR-0042, C2). Three constructed cases, with their closed forms in `engine/validation/closed_forms.py` and a test that checks them, keep CI covering a temperature change, mixed conditions, several materials and fixed-plus-variable failure; the engine, API and FEA tests that used course cases or course numbers use them instead.

AGENTS.md and the `project-rules` skill (0.2.0) forbid committing new ones; the moves and one history rewrite follow in chunks. The X840-Z60 material record stays. Risks R-06 and R-16 updated.
