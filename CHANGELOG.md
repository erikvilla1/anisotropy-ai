- The LaTeX manual covers the finite element layer: `docs/manual/build.py` reads `fea/docs/theory.md` with its code markers (listings from the `fea` source with real line numbers), solves every case in `fea/validation/cases` during the build, includes the newest report in `docs/evaluations/fea`, runs the `fea` tests beside the engine's, and prints both versions on the title page; a "Finite element layer" section and Bathe and Timoshenko references in `main.tex`. - `fea/` chunk 2, the membrane kernel: four-node plane-stress elements on the engine's laminate `A` matrix (2 by 2 Gauss), kinematic couplings as dof aliasing so a reference node's reaction is the sum over its region, prescribed displacements by partitioning, sparse LU solve with a singularity check, reactions, centroid and Gauss-point strains, section forces; refuses unsymmetric layups, free rigid-body motion, stray nodes and conflicting prescriptions.
