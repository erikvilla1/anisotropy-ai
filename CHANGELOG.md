### Fixed
- The result explanation names the ply stresses by their symbols (σ₁ along the fibres, σ₂ across, τ₁₂ in-plane shear), says they are element centroids in the ply's own axes and not the global-axis values a viewer shows, and no longer quotes a ply von Mises (ADR-0066), which the fact sheet also leaves out so no model can bring it back: the founder read a fibre stress named "S11" on the page, queried S11 in PrePoMax and found a value a hundred times smaller, because PrePoMax's S11 is σxx across the width (`fea/src/anisotropy_fea/explain.py`; the browser fixtures' explanation files are regenerated). In the validation table, on the page and in the coupon PDF, a component that is numerical noise beside the other two of its triple (below a billionth of the largest, such as a 4e-15 MPa shear in a 0° ply) prints as 0, so the columns no longer look different where they are equal (`lib/studio-colors.ts` `settled`, `docs/models/coupon.py`).
- A coupon whose structured mesh is past where the image's SPOOLES solver has failed gets a warning at the setup check, with the count and that a coarser mesh solves (ADR-0030 amendment 5: one 40 by 100 mesh of 24,000 layer bricks failed, 17,850 and an open-hole mesh of 25,000 solved, PaStiX solves all of them but the image has none); when CalculiX does stop, the job's message carries the step's own last words and our diagnosis of the known SPOOLES text, the solver's console is written beside the deck, and a stopped run's deck, console and status file are kept and stored. The setup check also warns when G13 or G23 is far from G12, which parts CalculiX's brick from our membrane.
- A hosted coupon job is no longer stopped as silent while its solver still works (ADR-0056 amendment 5): the solver's heartbeat writes the row alone, from the claim to the row's last write, the end's uploads included; the running job's files go to the bucket on a thread of their own; every database connection carries TCP keepalives and a 30 s user timeout, and the bucket client 10 s to connect and 30 s to read per try; the coupon folder's upload at a job's end sends only the files that changed.
- The coupon fact sheet keeps each measured value at the four significant figures the sentences print, so a model that copies the sheet no longer writes a number such as 16211.1968902878 N (ADR-0052 amendment 1, `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/explain.py`); the browser fixtures' explanation files carry the same digits.
- The workspace asks again for a job's status or its fields when one answer is missed (two seconds later, up to ten times per job; `components/studio/use-coupon-run.ts`): on the hosted site one read of the fields through the Worker went unanswered and the page stayed without results while the job was done. The browser stand-in fails the first read on purpose. The live site's findings of the evening are in `docs/evaluations/website/2026-11-05-beta-launch-checks.md`.

### Added
- ADR-0067: we build on open-source solvers instead of replacing them (the founder, 2026-11-09: "it is not something we are trying to replace but more build upon"). Our kernel keeps its scope, one linear static step on flat plates, as the fast path (0.35 to 0.42 s on the public unnotched coupon) and the independent check; every new physics (solids, nonlinear geometry, buckling eigenvalues and beyond) comes from CalculiX run as a separate program, and the composite layer reads either solver's results, so a job gives the same per-ply results, failure indices and document whichever solver solved it. The first work under it is making that layer solver-neutral; a kind only CalculiX solves says on the page that it has no independent check.
- The Abaqus column leaves the page's two tables (ADR-0066 amendment 1; the founder, 2026-11-08: "don't put abaqus in table, open source calculix is enough"): the Summary's validation table reads Laminate theory | Anisotropy | CalculiX and the peaks table Anisotropy | CalculiX, and the coupon document prints an Abaqus column only where the team's exported fields fill it. An Abaqus run of our own deck is read on the command line instead: `anisotropy_fea.abaqus_dat` reads the `.dat`'s printed tables as Abaqus 2025 lays them out (`*El Print` with section points, `*Node Print` with its TOTAL line), `anisotropy_fea.abaqus_summary` puts the reaction, the gauge strain (the section strains turned from the layup's axes to the coupon's) and every ply's σ₁ σ₂ τ₁₂ at the gauge element beside the kernel's with the record's 3 % rule, and `fea/tools/abaqus_beside.py` prints them; on the founder's run of `AS48552-UNT-L1` (2026-11-08) it read 28 of 28 values within tolerance, the reaction 4705 against 4706.8 N. The upload and the drop zone that were started for the column were dropped the same evening.
- ADR-0066: solver results are compared as one set of quantities (reaction, gauge-centre strain, every ply's σ₁ σ₂ τ₁₂ in the fibre axes at the gauge-centre element, a CLT column), reported in one table for CLT, Anisotropy, CalculiX and Abaqus with each column saying how its value was taken; the tab-line peaks go in a second table, per ply, labelled as a clamped-edge concentration that grows with the mesh; no composite von Mises. Written after the founder put the Summary, PrePoMax and Abaqus/CAE views of `AS48552-UNT-L1` side by side (2026-11-08) and the three were found to be one model read in three plies, frames and points: the same quantity read the same way agrees within 0.3 % (the record's evidence). Accepted the same day; the code changes are the three pull requests that follow (the `.frd` read per ply, the two tables on the page and in the package, the deck's section points).
- The Abaqus deck of a membrane coupon lists every layer's middle section point (3k − 1 at three Simpson points per layer) on its `S` and `E` `*El Print` tables, because Abaqus printed the two outer points alone and the validation table had plies 1 and 8 only; a rerun of the team coupon's deck printed every ply (ADR-0066, step 4; `fea/src/anisotropy_fea/write_abaqus.py`); a bending coupon keeps the faces. Every check deck is regenerated, and `tools/regenerate_decks.py --refresh-digests` rewrites a record's digest of a deck that changed in its output requests alone, which no solver's numbers depend on, and says when a deck changed beyond them and needs a new run.
- The coupon document prints the page's two tables (ADR-0066, step 3b): the verification chapter opens with the validation table (Quantity | Laminate theory | Anisotropy | CalculiX | Abaqus, with how each column's value is taken and where to find a cell in PrePoMax or Abaqus/CAE), filled from the same engine summary the page reads and from the record and the team's exported fields, and then the peaks table, per ply, titled "Stress concentration at the clamped edge: not a validation value" when a peak touches a grip, with the CalculiX corner node from the record's `[frd].plies`. The Abaqus export recipe records what the run of 2026-11-08 settled: a layer's `S11`, `S22`, `S12` are in the ply's axes, `E12` is the engineering shear strain, and `*El Print` without a section-point list writes the two outer points only (`docs/models/coupon.py`, `docs/abaqus-export-recipe.md`).
- The Summary shows two tables instead of a list of unlike numbers (ADR-0066, step 2). The validation table has one row per quantity (the reaction, the gauge-centre strain, every ply's σ₁ / σ₂ / τ₁₂ in its fibre axes at the gauge-centre element) and one column per source: laminate theory (`summary.clt`, new in `anisotropy_fea.fields`: the reaction over the width as N_y, N_x = N_xy = 0, each ply at its middle, through the engine's ABD; on the public coupon it meets the kernel's centre element within 0.1 %), Anisotropy at the element centroid, CalculiX as the integration-point average of the record (the gauge strain row is new, `summary.calculix.gauge_strain`), and Abaqus, empty until its field reports are uploaded; a header line under each column says how its value was taken. The peaks table, collapsed, holds every ply's largest σ₁ / σ₂ / τ₁₂ at the element centroids (`summary.ply_peaks`, each marked when its element touches a gripped node) beside CalculiX's corner-node values per ply from the `.frd`, under a title that says it is a stress concentration at the clamped edge and not a validation value; what PrePoMax shows in the global axes is one sentence below it. A job solved before this keeps the older list.
- The `.frd` is read per ply in the fibre axes (ADR-0066, step 1): `anisotropy_fea.frd` now reads the element block, assigns each brick's nodes to a ply by its mean z, and `frd_ply_peaks` rotates every ply's nodal state by its fibre angle (ADR-0065) and finds the ply's largest σ₁, σ₂ and |τ₁₂| with their nodes and positions, and the largest σ₁ in the gauge rows; `tools/record_from_dat.py` writes them as `plies`, an array of inline tables in the record's `[frd]` block, and `calculix_summary` carries them into the fields' units as `summary.calculix.frd.plies`. On `AS48552-UNT-L1` this puts CalculiX's 633.0 MPa corner-node value beside Anisotropy's 536.6 MPa centroid for the same 0° ply, and 363.8 beside 357.8 for the 45° ply whose face Abaqus/CAE shows at 332.8 with averaging. The page does not show them yet (step 2).
- The Summary adds first-ply failure in the gauge section: per criterion, the first ply to fail among the deck's `Gauge` elements that touch no gripped node (`anisotropy_fea.fields.gauge_failures`, `summary.fpf_gauge`), with its ply, mode and distance from the tab line, beside the model's own factor at the clamped edge (the public coupon: 1.032 by Tsai-Hill in the gauge against 1.013 at the grip edge). The founder asked (2026-11-08) whether the failure sits where a test coupon breaks; now the page says.
- The Summary shows CalculiX's numbers beside ours (ADR-0030 amendment 6): the reaction of the twin and our difference from it; the gauge section's ply stresses in the fibre axes, ours and CalculiX's, from the comparison record; and the peaks, ours at element centroids and CalculiX's as PrePoMax shows them (von Mises, SYY and SXX at nodes with their positions, from the `.frd`, read by `anisotropy_fea.frd`; the record gains an `[frd]` block). - A coupon job shows its result before the PDF (ADR-0064): `run_coupon.py` writes the result line to its progress as soon as the CalculiX record is written, then draws (`build_model.py --drawings only`, the engine's step now running `--drawings without`) and builds the package; the API's job status gains `result` and `documents` (`building`, then `written` or what is missing), the solver's heartbeat writes the result to the row, and the studio's job panel and chat show it while the files are made; `docker/check-image.py` reports when the result came (`result_seconds`). A job's row is now written before the process marks the job ended or queued again, which removes a race the job-queue tests hit about one run in four.
- Coupon solves run in parallel on the hosted tier (ADR-0056 amendments 3 and 4, stage 3 built early; ADR-0050 amendment 3): with the Worker's new `JOB_QUEUE` binding, the API's container files each job (`queued`, its input in R2) and the Worker sends it to the Cloudflare queue `anisotropy-coupon-jobs`; the Worker's queue consumer starts each job in a `SolverContainer` of its own (same image, `ANISOTROPY_JOB_ROLE=solver`, up to ten at once) and returns within seconds, so the next job starts at once; the solver's Durable Object looks at the job every 5 s until it ends, sends it back when another job holds its coupon and stops it at 12 minutes, and a job with no free solver waits in the queue (`worker/solver-queue.ts`). `run_coupon.py --gate` holds a run once its coupon is named; the solver claims the coupon there under a database lock, and migration 0008 admits one running job per coupon (a unique index), so two jobs never number the same attempt. ### Changed
- The Summary names its two solvers once ("Result: Anisotropy. The public check specs state `angles_from = "length"` with their layups as written, so their physical laminates turn by 90° and their decks and records are regenerated separately; the coupon input form is version 0.7 and a 0.6 values file is refused; `fea/tools/migrate_angles.py` rewrites an older spec's angles by minus 90° (the same physical laminate) and adds the key; the chats, the form's words, `fea/docs/theory.md` and `AGENTS.md` say so.
- Hosted coupon jobs start on a warm pool of solvers (ADR-0056 amendment 6): the Worker's consumer asks `solver-0` to `solver-9` in turn and the first free one takes the job (a busy solver answers 423), a finished job's container stays warm 15 minutes with its coupons on disk, the coupon folder's copy in the bucket leaves out the big run files every job used to download (`.frd`, `.dat`, `.vtu`, the fields, the PDF, the LaTeX, the package folders), the tool starts before the naming lock and holds twice at its gate so the lock covers one sync and the naming only, and CalculiX runs on every core. Every job's row (migration 0009: `claimed_at`, `timings`) and status carry the seconds of each stage and of the solver's restore, lock wait, gate and upload, shown beside the stages on the page; the container's peak memory falls back to the children's peak resident set where no cgroup file reads.
- The Files panel of a finished coupon job offers the package, its PDF, our Abaqus deck, the CalculiX twin and CalculiX's `.frd` for PrePoMax, and no longer lists the fields, the explanation, the setup check, the preview, the record, the engine's summary or the `.vtu` (`lib/studio-api.ts` `DOWNLOADS`; the founder, 2026-11-08: the JSON files mean nothing to a user).
